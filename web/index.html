How the final model picks 5 programmes

  1. Six simple methods each propose their top-50 programmes: H2 (the user's own completed history), EW (the same history, weighted by recency), PPR (a random walk over programmes watched together), MF (matrix factorisation), AR (association rules: viewers of X also watched Y) and popularity.
  2. A LightGBM ranker scores every proposed programme using 33 features (the strongest: the programme's rank in the user's own completed history).
  3. The five highest scores are shown. The small badges on the cards below list which methods had proposed that programme.
